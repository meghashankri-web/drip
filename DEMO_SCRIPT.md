# Drip: Demo Voice-Over Script (~3 minutes)

The parts in **[brackets]** are what's happening on screen. Everything else is what you say.

---

## 1. Inspiration (~25s)
**[Welcome screen]**

> I was the first in my family to go to college in the US. I showed up not knowing anyone. In college, everyone tells you to network, but nobody tells you who to ask, or how. First-generation women all over the country feel this. We don't have family connections to start with, and mentors, networks and good information aren't spread evenly. So I built **Drip**, where women pour it forward.

## 2. Purpose (~20s)

> Drip does the part nobody teaches you. It suggests women nearby who can teach you something, and women who want to learn what you already know. It helps you ask in your own words, gets the chat on the calendar, and celebrates it afterwards. The goal is for every coffee chat to help both people.

## 3. Sign up with Claude (~35s)
**[Sign-up form → resume upload]**

> Signing up takes a few minutes. I upload my resume, and Claude reads it and drafts my summary, my experience tags and my career stage. These are what I can *offer* others. I stay in control of every word.

**[Interests: tapping suggestions, reordering]**

> Next I pick up to five things I want to *learn*. Claude suggests them based on my background and leans toward topics other members can actually help with. I rank them, and that order drives my matches.

**[Personality questions → Create account]**

> A few questions about me, how I like to meet, and how many chats I want each month. And I'm in.

## 4. Feed & matching (~30s)
**[Feed]**

> My feed ranks women by how close they are and how well their experience matches what I want to learn. This banner tells me exactly why. She can help with my top interests, and she's minutes away. I can swipe left to pass, or…

**[Coffee? → "Why you two" panel loads]**

> …swipe right. Claude reads both profiles and her resume, live, and tells me what we have in common and which of *her* projects relate to *my* goals. So I'm never walking in cold. That's the part that makes asking less awkward.

**[Typing a message, choosing times, Send]**

## 5. Connecting (~30s)
**[Switching to her profile → Messages]**

> Now I'm her. She sees my note and when I'm free. She can say Not now, which politely tells me she's busy and that Drip will circle back in a month. Or she can accept…

**[Accept → date, time, café → Confirm → Add to Google Calendar]**

> …pick a time and place, and it goes straight onto her Google Calendar.

**[Switching back → Messages]**

> Back as me: she said yes, and it's on my calendar too.

## 6. Ripples (~25s)
**[Simulate meeting passed → Yes → photo + kind words → Post → Profile]**

> After the chat, Drip asks one question: did it happen? Only real coffee chats count. That's what makes a **ripple**. It's not a follower or a friend request. It's proof two women actually helped each other. My ripple count goes up, and the post lives on my profile. Other members can cheer it, but only I see how many cheers I got, so it's about gratitude, not popularity.

## 7. Close (~15s)

> Drip turns "go network" into one real conversation at a time. Every chat helps both people, and every ripple grows a stronger network of women, especially for those of us who had to start from zero.

## Next Steps (for the submission form)

1. **Pilot with first-generation programs.** Partner with university first-gen offices, TRIO programs, and scholarship networks such as QuestBridge to seed communities where the need is greatest.
2. **Reach women everywhere.** Drip already supports Zoom chats. Next we'll make it an installable app that works offline and on low bandwidth, add multilingual sign-up (Claude can already read resumes in many languages), and match across cities and countries for women far from professional hubs.
3. **Complete the ripple experience.** Build a Ripple feed of your connections' coffee chats with private Cheers, reminders to follow up, and nudges to "pour it forward" by offering a chat once you've received one.
4. **Deeper AI help.** Use Claude to prep both people before a chat (conversation starters and questions to ask), suggest follow-up resources, and recommend the next person to meet based on what you learned.
5. **Trust and safety.** Real sign-in, identity and age verification, reporting and moderation, and privacy controls for location and Race/Culture.
6. **Measure impact.** Track completed ripples, repeat connections, and outcomes such as internships, jobs and new roles, so schools and partners can see opportunity spreading.
